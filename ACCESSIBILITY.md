# Accessibility

ARICT aims to make its website and project community usable by as many people as possible. Accessibility is part of our definition of a complete contribution.

## Our approach

We aim to:

- Use semantic HTML, meaningful headings, labels, landmarks, and link text.
- Support keyboard navigation and visible focus states.
- Provide useful alternative text for informative images.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Maintain readable contrast, responsive layouts, and clear form errors.
- Avoid communicating important information through colour alone.

## Reporting an accessibility issue

If you encounter an accessibility barrier, please contact the ARICT administrators through [arict.lk/contact](https://arict.lk/contact). Include the page, the task you were trying to complete, the assistive technology or browser involved if relevant, and any suggested improvement. Please do not include personal or sensitive information.

We welcome specific, respectful reports and will prioritise barriers that prevent people from accessing core content or completing important tasks.

## Contributing accessibly

When changing the website, test the affected flow with a keyboard and at a narrow viewport. Preserve semantic structure, focus management, reduced-motion behaviour, and accessible names. Add or update automated tests when the repository has coverage for the affected behaviour.
