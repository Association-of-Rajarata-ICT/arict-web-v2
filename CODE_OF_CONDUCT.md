# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

ARICT is committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or role.

We expect participants to:

- Treat each other with respect and empathy.
- Give constructive feedback and accept it in good faith.
- Credit other people's work and ideas.
- Focus on what is best for the community.
- Help keep discussions, events, and contributions inclusive.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, and publishing private information without permission.

## Reporting concerns

Repository administrators accept content reports. Report a concern privately to the ARICT administrators through the contact details on [arict.lk](https://arict.lk/contact), or through the repository's private reporting channel where available. Do not open a public issue for sensitive reports.

Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical. Retaliation against anyone who makes a good-faith report is not tolerated.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies to repository spaces and community activities, and when someone is representing ARICT in public spaces. Administrators may remove content, restrict participation, or take other action they consider appropriate after reviewing a report.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
